Library ASBA

Library Management System

Every loan, copy and fine in one place.

Library ASBA handles the desk work — issuing and returning books, tracking overdue fines and keeping the catalogue accurate — without a server to maintain.

  • Circulation Issue, renew and return with automatic due dates and fines.
  • Catalogue Titles, individual copies, shelf locations and reservations.
  • Reporting Overdue lists, borrowing trends and outstanding balances.
